# Mi Ojo OS demo

Investor demo for Ciarra Pardo's Mi Ojo OS \u2014 a founder-led lifestyle platform across Mi Ojo (creative & brand studio), BeautyDays (community), Peppers & Beli (hospitality), and Residences (wellness stays). AI is positioned as the operating layer that scales the founder, not replaces her.

Design system: Pharos V9 cream-shell editorial. Cream backgrounds, white raised cards, teal accent, gold CTAs, editorial hairline + JetBrains Mono eyebrows. Never federal or generic SaaS.

Crew config: `lib/miojo.ts`. Mock data: `data/miojo-mock.ts`. Crew pages: `app/crews/<slug>/page.tsx`.
